## 🎤 2. Word-by-Word Pitch Script (With Screen Actions)

### 🚀 Phase 1: Opening Hook (0:00 - 0:45)
> **What to Say:**
> *"Good morning respected judges and panel members. We are **Team CodeXtra**, and today we present **EcoDispose** — a Smart EV Battery Disposal & Circular Recycling Ecosystem.*
> 
> *India and the world are rapidly adopting Electric Vehicles. But there is a silent ticking time bomb: **What happens when an EV battery ages?** Today, EV owners have zero transparent diagnostic tools. As a result, batteries with 70% life left are either sold for pennies or discarded into the unregulated informal sector. Even worse, physically compromised or overheated packs cause catastrophic thermal runaway fires and toxic groundwater pollution.*
> 
> *Our solution? A unified, transparent digital bridge connecting **Battery Owners**, **Vehicle Repair Workshops**, **Stationary Energy Storage Buyers**, and **Certified Hazmat Recyclers**."*

> 3. *Tier 3 (Score < 40 or Structural Damage): **Certified Hazmat Recyclers** — Hydrometallurgical recovery of scarce Lithium, Cobalt, and Nickel.*
> 
> *Let us walk you through the live platform."*

## ❓ 3. Top Judge Questions & Winning Answers (Viva / Q&A Prep)

### Q1: "Why rule-based scoring instead of an AI/ML model?"
> **Answer:**  
> *"In regulatory and safety-critical domains like hazardous lithium battery transport, **auditability and explainability are mandatory**. A black-box neural network cannot legally explain to an insurer or safety board why a battery was condemned.*  
> *Our rule-based engine provides an exact, auditable deduction breakdown. However, our architecture is modular: as real fleet impedance spectroscopy (EIS) data is collected, machine learning models (like Random Forests or LSTMs) can easily plug in as the scoring backend."*

---

### Q2: "How do you verify that the battery owner isn't entering fake data in the form?"
> **Answer:**  
> *"In our deployment roadmap, manual entry is only the preliminary screening layer. The binding custody stage occurs at Step 3: **'Tested & Audited'**.*  
> *When the certified workshop collects the pack, they attach a diagnostic OBD-II or BMS flasher to verify actual internal resistance and capacity against the owner's self-declaration before releasing funds."*

---

### Q3: "What is the commercial revenue model for EcoDispose?"
> **Answer:**  
> *"EcoDispose operates on a three-pronged revenue model:*  
> 1. *A **3–5% transaction commission** on successful repair jobs and 2nd-life storage battery sales.*  
> 2. *A **SaaS subscription fee** for verified vendor workshops and recycling plants for inbound lead generation.*  
> 3. *An **EPR Compliance API service** charging EV OEMs and fleet operators (like Ola, Uber, BluSmart) to generate certified auditable battery disposal manifests."*

---

### Q4: "How did you calculate the CO₂ avoided numbers?"
> **Answer:**  
> *"Our CO₂ calculations are rooted in peer-reviewed Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) benchmarks:*  
> * *Manufacturing a new 40 kWh EV pack emits approximately 2,800 to 3,200 kg of CO₂.*  
> * *By repairing and repacking a module, we offset new battery manufacturing, saving ~**420 kg CO₂**.*  
> * *By repurposing a pack into solar storage, we displace diesel generator peaking, saving ~**310 kg CO₂**.*  
> * *Closed-loop recycling offsets virgin lithium and cobalt mining emissions, saving ~**160 kg CO₂**."*
